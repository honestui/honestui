Our icons bring Honest UI's source-first approach to interface symbols. Each icon is open source, composable, and designed to fit naturally into modern React applications.

They share a consistent visual system and can be imported directly from `honestui/icons`.

## Why our icons?

Icons make actions and information easier to recognize, but mixed styles can make an interface feel uneven. Our icons provide a broad set of symbols with consistent sizing, proportions, and React APIs.

Every icon is:

- **Composable.** Use icons inside buttons, menus, fields, charts, and your own components.
- **Themeable.** Icons inherit `currentColor`, so they follow text, state, and theme colors.
- **Tree-shakeable.** Named imports let your bundler keep only the icons your application uses.
- **Source-first.** Inspect the SVG output, wrap the component, or adapt it for your product.

## Built for product interfaces

The collection includes interface controls, arrows, files, commerce, brands, charts, devices, status symbols, and more. Default, filled, rounded, and doodle variants are available where the concept supports them.

Every icon forwards standard SVG props and includes typed metadata for its name, category, variant, tags, and view box.

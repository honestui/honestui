## Overview

Orbiting Circles spaces its children evenly around a circle and turns them slowly around the center. Use it to show integrations, team members, or supported platforms around a central product. Render several with different radii, durations, and directions inside one box to build concentric rings. Items stay upright as they travel.

## Installation

<CliBlock commands={["orbiting-circles"]} />

## Usage

```tsx
import { OrbitingCircles } from "@/components/animated/orbiting-circles"

<div className="relative flex h-80 items-center justify-center">
  <span>Integrations</span>
  <OrbitingCircles radius={64} iconSize={30}>
    <GithubIcon />
    <SlackIcon />
  </OrbitingCircles>
  <OrbitingCircles radius={128} reverse duration={30}>
    …
  </OrbitingCircles>
</div>
```

The orbit is centered in its nearest positioned ancestor. Keep that box at least `2 × radius + iconSize` wide and tall, or clip it with `overflow-hidden`.

## Accessibility

The ring is decorative. Items remain in the document and keep their own accessible names, but moving targets are hard to click, so avoid putting the only path to an action on an orbit. When reduced motion is requested, items stop in their evenly spaced positions.
